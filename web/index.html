How to play

  • ← → Move
  • ↑ Jump
  • Space Fire
  • Esc Quit game

Load the game, wait for the title screen, then click it to start. Hold the keys to move and fire. A desktop keyboard is recommended.

Use the volume slider to adjust all sound; set it to 0 to mute. The background song plays quietly beneath the effects. Disable music turns off just the song.

Explore three levels, shoot the floating band-member faces to open the way forward, and survive to the end. Finish quickly and conserve ammunition: every second costs 10 points, while each unused shot earns 1,000 bonus points.
