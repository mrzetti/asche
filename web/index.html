How to play

  • ← → Move
  • ↑ Jump
  • Space Fire
  • Esc Quit game

Load the game, wait for the title screen, then tap it to start. Hold the keys or on-screen buttons to move and fire. Touch controls appear automatically on touch devices; you can also toggle them above. Hold movement and jump or fire together.

Use the volume slider to adjust the original game audio; set it to 0 to mute.

Explore three levels, shoot the floating band-member faces to open the way forward, and survive to the end. Finish quickly and conserve ammunition: every second costs 10 points, while each unused shot earns 1,000 bonus points.

Original game by Oliver Czok for Motor Music, © 1997. Preserved from the Rammstein World download. Game rights belong to their respective owners.

Runs locally in your browser with Boxedwine and Wine. The first load downloads the emulator and its runtime.
